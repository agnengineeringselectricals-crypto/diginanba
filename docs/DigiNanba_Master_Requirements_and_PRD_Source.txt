1. VISION AND BUSINESS MODEL
- Brand/domain: DigiNanba, diginanba.com. GitHub: agnengineeringselectricals-crypto/diginanba.
- Build a global digital-product marketplace where people discover, create, sell, buy and use useful digital products.
- Serve individuals, homemakers, students, freelancers, professionals, small businesses, creators, organizations and institutions.
- Support creators publishing products/tools and earning. Creators can set local-market prices; customers should see suitable local currency.
- Owner is based in India and uses an Indian INR bank account. Design currency conversion, settlement, provider fees, taxes and cross-border payouts accordingly; validate details before launch.
- Long-term ambition: reach at least 20% of each country's population as accounts. This is an aspiration, not a forecast.
- Select the first two target countries based on evidence of digital-product demand and revenue potential. Record the final decision and rationale.

2. GLOBALIZATION AND LOCALIZATION
- Support national and relevant local languages across countries.
- Localize suitable high-demand/trending products into local languages, subject to quality, rights, accuracy and safety checks.
- Display local currency, support creator local-currency pricing and reliable conversion.
- Market detection must not assume all users are in the US. Use reliable signals and allow manual country/market override.
- Header preference: replace US/UK currency labels with a two-letter language/country indicator and flag (Amazon-like), with sign-in and cart nearby.
- Historical issue: market selection was temporary homepage state; some pages defaulted to US unless passed in URL. Earlier detection recognized UK locale/time zone otherwise defaulted US. Unify and test market behavior.
- Prevent mixed-market cart/checkout errors and clearly handle currency compatibility.

3. AI PRODUCT DISCOVERY AND FACTORY
- Internal AI agents should research regional demand/trends, discover product needs, create digital-product drafts, localize and improve them.
- Do not rely only on sellers submitting products; factory should proactively draft products based on demand.
- All AI-created products remain drafts until quality review, testing and explicit publication approval. Never auto-publish.
- Review originality/IP rights, accuracy, localization, safety, usability and completeness.
- Internal agent features, prompts, job queues, agent names and controls must not leak into customer pages. The user explicitly said internal agent-only “+save” functionality must not be shown to customers.
- Aim for one controlled factory job/workflow, avoid duplicate jobs and unnecessary tables/helpers. Inspect dependencies/backups before cleanup.
- Control AI spending, rate limits, retries and queues. API credits were previously exhausted and tasks received HTTP 429. Do not assume paid API credits exist or trigger paid usage without authorization. Support offline scaffolding where practical.
- Maintain human review/correction/testing and a report before publication.

4. CATALOG AND TAXONOMY
- Broad, extensible categories include ebooks/guides; Excel/Google Sheets templates, trackers and calculators; business/finance templates; productivity/planners; education/student resources; freelancer/professional tools; design/creative assets; marketing/sales resources; interactive tools and web utilities.
- Allow new product types/categories to be added.
- Product records should include unique ID, title, short useful description, category/type, creator/author name and creator ID, market/pricing, language and searchable metadata.
- Search by product ID, title, description and creator/author; creator ID should be available to relevant workflows.
- Existing demo products can receive suitable IDs; attribute AI-created products to DigiNanba only when that is accurate.
- Demand/search records should support continent > region > country > state > district hierarchy.

5. BUYER AND CREATOR EXPERIENCE
- Buyers: browse, search, product detail, cart, purchase and access/download entitlements.
- Creators: role-appropriate workspace for creating/editing products, managing drafts/listings and viewing earnings.
- Routine editor operations (typing, formatting, colors, layout, formulas, charts from existing data) should not trigger unnecessary AI calls.
- Keep in-progress drafts isolated from published products and production assets until an explicit validated lifecycle action.
- Account/settings experience should be attractive and easy to use, with Amazon.in-like usability.
- Account pages must provide real purchase history and download/access fulfillment; earlier versions had placeholders.

6. PROFESSIONAL-CATEGORY PERSONALIZATION
- Homepage recommendations should adapt to profession/life situation. Explicit examples: freelancers see freelancer-relevant products; school students see student-relevant products.
- Suggested starting categories: Freelancer/Independent professional; School student; College/University student; Teacher/Educator; Small business owner/Entrepreneur; Homemaker; Employee/Corporate professional; Developer/IT professional; Designer/Creative professional; Marketing/Sales professional; Finance/Accounting professional; Engineer/Technical professional; Healthcare professional; Organization/Institution; Job seeker; Researcher; Other.
- Add suitable profile fields during signup/account setup.
- First-time user may skip and browse. Continue to prompt users who have not chosen a basic category, with a considerate cadence.
- Persist category in authenticated user's database profile, not only localStorage. Allow edits in account settings and sync across sessions/devices.
- Recommendations should consider profile, search intent, market, language and product metadata; simple keyword reordering is only an interim implementation.
- Historical implementation used localStorage/sessionStorage and keyword ranking on a limited fetched set; this is incomplete until database-backed and properly tested.

8. IDENTITY, AUTH AND SECURITY
- Support credentials login/signup and Google sign-in where enabled.
- Password reset should support email/phone and two-step verification as appropriate, with secure tokens and expiry.
- System-generated unique account/user ID must not be editable by user/creator.
- User's proposed ID concept: country first 2 or 3 letters; total length described as 8 digits, with remaining 5 or 6 digits used for uniqueness; user also considered alphanumeric base36/base62. Final exact format, collision strategy and migration must be decided and documented.
- Record IP/session information for security/audit only where lawful and proportionate; define purpose, retention and privacy. Do not expose IP to other users or permit editing system-generated audit fields.
- Protect passwords, sessions, reset tokens and personal data. Never expose API keys, passwords or database URLs in chat, source or public repositories.

9. PAYMENTS, ORDERS, REFUNDS AND PAYOUTS
- Build complete payment/order lifecycle, provider verification, secure price revalidation, signed webhook verification, idempotency and correct handling of duplicate/out-of-order events.
- Razorpay was configured/discussed. Never expose secrets; use provider dashboard environment variables.
- Earlier checkout created pending order/order-item rows but was demo mode without live provider session/payment record. Verify current state.
- Never enable real-money collection without explicit user approval, even if keys are configured.
- Build order history/statuses, cancellations/refunds, disputes/support, creator earnings and payouts, currency conversion, fees, tax/invoice handling.
- Grant downloads only after verified payment; reconcile entitlements for refunds/chargebacks.
- Validate INR settlement, cross-border rules, fees, taxes, payout timing and provider availability before launch. Never trust client price/payment status.

10. DIGITAL DELIVERY AND PRODUCT LIFECYCLE
- Paid products must appear in buyer account with authorized access/download.
- Protect downloads with authorization, expiring/signed URLs or equivalent controls, rate limits and audit trails.
- Distinguish draft, review, approved, published, suspended/withdrawn and archived states.
- Version products; do not silently overwrite purchased/approved versions.
- Provide license/usage terms and support for digital delivery issues.

11. SEARCH, DISCOVERY AND ANALYTICS
- Search product ID/title/description/creator and relevant metadata.
- Use geographic hierarchy and language/market for demand research and recommendations.
- Track demand responsibly using privacy-conscious aggregation and retention.
- Measure acquisition, engagement, conversion, retention, revenue, refunds, creator outcomes, search success and product quality. Never fabricate popularity/sales.

12. ADMIN AND OPERATIONS

- Keep one controlled AI factory workflow; inspect dependencies/backups before deleting jobs, tables or helper files. Never casually delete production data.
- Add budget controls, queue monitoring, retry/backoff and failure reporting.
