# DigiNanba Promote & Earn Policy — Launch Requirement

**Status:** Proposed policy; not active until reviewed, configured, tested, and expressly approved by DigiNanba.

## 1. Purpose and scope

DigiNanba may reward eligible people and organizations for genuine, compliant promotion of DigiNanba and its digital products. Participation is open to approved individuals, creators, publishers, educators, communities, agencies, and business partners, subject to applicable law and the relevant program terms.

## 2. Platform-neutral promotion

Promoters may use any lawful social media platform, messaging service, website, blog, newsletter, podcast, forum, online community, QR code, event, or other legitimate marketing channel worldwide. Examples include YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, X (formerly Twitter), LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram, Pinterest, Reddit, Snapchat, TikTok where available, and platforms introduced in the future. This list is illustrative and non-exhaustive; DigiNanba does not restrict eligibility to named platforms.

Promoters must follow each channel's rules and applicable advertising, privacy, consumer-protection, and anti-spam laws. No unsolicited bulk messaging, misleading claims, impersonation, fake engagement, cookie stuffing, malware, or deceptive redirects are permitted. Required sponsorship/affiliate disclosures must be clear and conspicuous.

## 3. Proposed program types

These are planning defaults only, not a promise of commission or a currently active offer. Final rates must be approved after margin, tax, payment, and fraud review.

- **Affiliate Partner:** proposed 10% of eligible net product revenue; up to 15% for approved tiers/campaigns.
- **Refer a Friend:** proposed customer first-order discount of 5% and referrer reward of 5% of eligible net revenue, subject to anti-abuse controls.
- **Creator/Influencer Partner:** proposed 10–20% of eligible net revenue or a separately approved fixed-fee campaign.
- **Educator/Community Ambassador:** proposed 10% of eligible net revenue or a pre-approved fixed campaign fee.
- **Business/Distribution Partner:** proposed 15–25% only under an approved agreement and margin review.
- **Performance/Seasonal Rewards:** milestone bonuses or prizes only under published, funded campaign rules.
- **Regional and language campaigns:** optional time-limited incentives for approved markets and languages.

No participant earns simply by recruiting another promoter. Rewards must be tied to qualifying sales or documented, legitimate promotional services. No guaranteed-income claims are permitted.

## 4. Attribution and eligible revenue

Each promoter must have a unique account/ID and trackable referral link or campaign code. Attribution must be recorded with the relevant click/campaign, product, order, and policy version. The initial proposal is a 30-day last eligible referral-click window, but this must be tested and approved before activation.

Eligible net revenue must be explicitly defined in the customer/partner terms. The approved calculation should account for discounts, indirect taxes where applicable, refunds, cancellations, chargebacks, and other clearly disclosed exclusions. Commission must not be calculated on tax collected on behalf of authorities. Multiple promoters must not receive duplicate commission for one sale unless an approved campaign explicitly defines split attribution.

## 5. Qualification, validation, and payout

- Only legitimate, completed, eligible transactions count.
- Commissions remain pending through the defined refund/fraud review period.
- Refunds, cancellations, chargebacks, and confirmed fraud reverse or adjust the associated reward under published terms.
- Proposed payout cadence: monthly after reconciliation.
- Proposed minimum payout: INR 1,000 or an approved equivalent in the recipient's payout currency; balances carry forward.
- Payout methods depend on recipient country, verification, provider support, and applicable law.
- Collect only necessary identity, payout, and tax information with appropriate access controls and privacy notices.
- Provide a transparent ledger of pending, approved, paid, reversed, and disputed rewards, plus an appeal/support process.
- Do not activate payouts until settlement, reconciliation, tax handling, and accounting have been tested.

## 6. Fraud, compliance, and enforcement

Prohibited activity includes self-referrals where disallowed, duplicate/fake accounts, bots, fabricated purchases, stolen content, spam, false endorsements, unauthorized brand bidding where restricted, misleading pricing/earnings claims, and circumvention of attribution controls. DigiNanba may investigate, suspend tracking or payouts, reverse invalid rewards, and terminate participation according to clear terms and applicable law. Disputed amounts must be handled under a documented process, and participants should have a reasonable way to appeal.

## 7. Required website and operational features

Before launch of this program, implement and verify:

- Public program overview, eligibility, terms, disclosures, prohibited conduct, and payout policy.
- Promoter application, acceptance of the applicable version of terms, and admin approval where required.
- Unique referral links/codes and platform-neutral campaign tracking.
- Server-side attribution and protection against tampering and duplicate events.
- Commission ledger tied to orders, refund events, chargebacks, and policy versions.
- Admin configuration for approved rates, categories, markets, campaigns, caps, and effective dates.
- Promoter dashboard for clicks, qualifying sales, pending/approved/reversed commissions, payouts, and downloadable statements.
- Payout verification, reconciliation, audit trail, support, dispute, and appeal workflows.
- Privacy-conscious analytics and retention controls.
- Tests for normal sales, repeat visits, cross-device limitations, refunds, duplicate webhooks, fraud signals, currency conversion, and payout failures.

## 8. Governance and launch gate

This policy is a launch-planning requirement, not evidence that the program has been implemented. Do not advertise commissions, accept participants into an active paid program, accrue payable rewards, or issue payouts until the business owner approves final rates and terms, relevant legal/tax review is complete, the required features are implemented, and end-to-end tests pass. All rates and thresholds above are proposals and may be changed before activation. No live payment collection or public product publishing is enabled by this policy.

## 9. Review

Review the policy when entering a new country, changing payout providers, changing attribution logic, introducing a new reward type, or when applicable law/platform rules change.
